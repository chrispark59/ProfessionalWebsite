About

Hi! My name is Chris Park and I'm a design engineer at the USC.

To give some background information on myself, my interests are in world building, spatial computing, and fitness. When I was younger I loved watching movies and playing sandbox video games which later translated to an strong interest in 3D modeling, environment design, and making 3D animations.

I got accepted into USC as a Game Art major but I immediately attempted to transfer into USC's computer science program because I thought it was the "safer" bet for my future. Ironically, I actually did enjoy the thought process and ownernship that engineering provided I just didn't know what type of engineering I was interested in and I didn't want to give up art. In my sophomore year at USC my transfer request got rejected and for the first time my GPA wasn't the only thing that mattered in my life. To find some sense of purpose I joined SEP (a startup incubator) on a whim and met some of the most intelligent people I have ever met in my life. This community showed me that genuine passion in what you do always beats doing something for status and a paycheck. For now I have focussed on spatial computing and AI engineering because it aligns with my interests, but I am simply chasing whatever I'm actually interested in so this will definitely change over time.
