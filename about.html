About

Hi! My name is Chris Park and I'm a design engineer at the USC.

To give some background information on myself, my interests are in world building and spatial computing. When I was younger I loved watching movies and playing sandbox video games which later translated to a strong interest in 3D modeling, environment design, and making 3D animations.

With my interest in games and movies, I initially came to USC intent on becoming a video game programmer but over time I developed a stronger interest in AI agents and spatial computing technologies.
