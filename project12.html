Adaptive Physical Therapy

APT was my final project for my Extended Reality Technologies course at USC. Throughout the span of 5 weeks, my team and I built an at home physical therapy experience in the Apple Vision Pro. I self taught myself how to develop using Polyspatial SDK through reverse engineering Unity starter scenes and constant iteration.

Skills

  • Apple Vision Pro
  • Vision OS
  • Polyspatial SDK
  • Unity
  • Swift

Problem Space

Home rehabilitation today is confusing, isolating, and rarely provides the real-time feedback patients need to progress; patients are also slow to recover
