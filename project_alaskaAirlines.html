Alaska Airlines Internship

This summer I had the privilege of interning on the Alaska Airlines Maintenance & Engineering(M&E) division specifically in the technical training team. I developed a proof of concept virtual reality simulation (digitizing their pre-departure safety check procedure) using the Pico 4 headset. Working as a one person team, I was challenged with handling everything from technical development to environment design. For development I used Unity and developed the code with Claude, with me focussing on overall architecture and planning and Claude executing. I designed a lot of the UI flows using Figma and used Blender to develop 3D models of certain aircraft parts.

Problem Space

My team's job is to teach incoming maintenance technicians how to operate on Alaska planes. The problem with the current approach is that they rely on powerpoint presentation and computer based training. Both of these methods lack the hands-on experience that they roles require and are lengthy. On the other hand there is on-the-job training where instructors teach on live aircraft. This method is flawed because sometimes planes simply aren't available to train with or the model of plane that is needed isn't around in the hangar.

Guided Mode

Users are given the abiity to use 2 modes within the simulation which are the guided and practice mode. Guided mode, which is demonstrated in the video above, acts as an instructional course on how to do the pre-departure safety checklist procedure. The user has access to an instruction manual that can be activated by a left controller grip click that shows them the steps of how to complete the procedure. Visually the user is also guided by a shader that is activated on the relevant part of the aircraft.
